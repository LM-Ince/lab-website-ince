O’Brien is a sophomore Neuroscience major at Pomona. In his free time, he enjoys playing classical guitar, fly fishing, and freediving in the kelp forests of the Pacific Northwest. He is interested in how neuroinflammation and circadian rhythms interact to influence mental health. His current research focuses on how IL-6 mechanistically affects VIP signaling in the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN), the brain’s central clock.
