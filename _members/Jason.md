Jason is a very passionate badminton player, and a casual vocalist/guitarist. He is currently a senior at Pomona College majoring in neuroscience. For his senior project, he is working on identifying circadian expression patterns of the mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) in the mouse hippocampus, and its potential implications in memory. His current research interests lie in the molecular mechanisms of memory formation and its interactions with time-of-day.
